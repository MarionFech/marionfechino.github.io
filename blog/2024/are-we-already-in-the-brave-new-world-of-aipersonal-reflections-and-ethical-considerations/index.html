Are we already in the Brave New World of AI? — Personal Reflections and Ethical Considerations

✨ Why Read This? ✨

  • Gain personal insights into balancing professional aspirations with ethical considerations.
  • Understand the implications of our society’s shift from mass production to hyper-personalization and how AI amplifies that.
  • Reflect on your role in shaping ethical AI practices and creating a sustainable future.

Let’s ignite meaningful conversations and inspire positive change in the AI landscape.

Today, I had a chain of little moments that made me reflect deeply on my professional journey and its impact on my daughter. The day began with a reflective walk in the 27°C heat at 8 am, dropping my daughter off at kindergarten. This walk, filled with eco-anxiety — thanks to Montreal’s current heat wave, made me realize the importance of finding sustainable and fulfilling work to inspire her, demonstrating strength and confidence by example. I asked myself how I wanted to contribute to the future society she will be a part of.

Back home, a routine conversation with a bank representative unexpectedly turned into a profound discussion about finding meaningful employment. Her straightforward advice struck a chord with me. Later, during my daily content review, I watched a webinar on using AI to automate time-consuming tasks. While insightful, it was clearly a sales pitch, and I was dismayed to discover a tracker installed in my Google account afterward. Not cool.

In parallel, I was developing an AI tool to organize files — an exciting project that helps streamline digital clutter. Thus, it is totally locally on your machine (a critical point if you read this article to the end!). Meanwhile, I listened to a podcast by Asma Mhalla (Yes, you could use an AI tool if you feel that the podcast is too long and you want a summary or because it is in French, I let you check the many possible apps, tools, and their friends developed for that already), which profoundly impacted me. Mhalla discussed our society’s shift from mass production to hyper-personalization, where we are individually targeted yet treated with the same algorithms.

Even more unsettling is how well Google knows us. We have no filter with it, no need to impress or lie. We are constantly bombarded with ads, monitored, and mapped out like discovered territories. Numerous apps now use AI to assist us with everything — from voicing WhatsApp messages to responding to emails and correcting our grammar. While these tools save time and are beneficial when used well, we give these apps everything. Isn’t that concerning?

This brings me to a crucial point: AI tools can significantly enhance our lives by saving time and increasing efficiency, but this convenience comes at the cost of our data privacy.

Free AI is not viable. Large language models require massive storage, and each query consumes significant energy and space. Companies aren’t offering these services for free out of goodwill; they are lucrative enterprises seeking profit. They will eventually monetize through data sales or subscription fees. As data scientists and AI enthusiasts, we must be vigilant about whom we trust with our information.

AI holds incredible potential for positive impact, but it also poses ethical challenges that we cannot ignore. We need to consider our role in this rapidly evolving landscape carefully. As professionals, it’s our responsibility to navigate these complexities and advocate for ethical AI practices.

Let’s take a moment to reflect on our journey in this exciting yet daunting field. How can we contribute to a future where AI serves humanity ethically and responsibly with sustainability? Your thoughts and insights are invaluable in shaping this dialogue.

Feel free to share your experiences and join the conversation. Together, we can pave the way for a more ethical and inspiring AI-driven future!
